Overview

Jim Grass brings over 25 years of legal experience, advising C-suite executives and business leaders in global public and private companies. Recognized for his strategic insight, he specializes in corporate law, real estate transactions, contract negotiation, and risk management, delivering legal solutions that mitigate risks and drive growth.

As Vice President of Corporate Counsel at Boldyn Networks (formerly Mobilitie), he successfully led teams in executing mergers, acquisitions, and refinancing transactions valued at over $600 million. His negotiation skills have fostered strategic deals with corporations like Amazon and Google, along with significant legal cost reductions through operational efficiency and litigation management.
