Harborview Law, P.C. (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, harborviewlaw.com (the “Website”), in compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA).

**Information We Collect**

We collect the following categories of personal information:

**Identifiers:** Name, email address, phone number, IP address, and account details.

- **Commercial Information:** Records of products or services purchased.
- **Internet Activity:** Browsing history, search history, and interactions with our Website.
- **Geolocation Data:** Approximate location based on IP address.
- **Inferences:** Preferences and interests derived from your usage of the Website.

**How We Use Your Information**

We use your personal information to:

- Provide and improve our services.
- Personalize your experience on our Website.
- Respond to inquiries and customer service requests.
- Send promotional communications and updates, if you’ve opted in.
- Detect and prevent fraudulent or illegal activities.

**Sharing Your Information**

We may share your information with:

- **Service Providers:** For business purposes such as payment processing, data analysis, and email delivery.
- **Legal Obligations:** If required to comply with laws or respond to legal requests.
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, sale, or other transfer of assets.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

**Your Rights Under the CCPA**

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

- **Right to Know:** Request details about the personal information we collect, use, and share.
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt-out of the sale of your personal information (if applicable).
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** Exercise your rights without facing discrimination.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@harborviewlaw.com.

**Your Choices**

- **Cookies and Tracking:** You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.
